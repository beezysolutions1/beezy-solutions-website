Who we are

Beezy Solutions (“Beezy”, “we”, “us”) is a branding, website development and digital marketing agency. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it and the choices you have. For any privacy question, write to info@beezysolutions.com.

Information you give us

  • Enquiry form. Your name, phone number, business name, the service you need and your message. The form does not send this to our servers. It prepares a WhatsApp message or email, and you choose whether to send it.
  • When you contact us. Whatever you share with us on WhatsApp, email, phone or social media.
  • When you become a client. Billing details, business information, brand material and access to accounts (such as ad accounts, website or social media) needed to deliver your project.

Information collected automatically

  • This website remembers your language and theme choice in your own browser. This stays on your device and is not sent to us.
  • Fonts on this website are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address to deliver them.
  • We do not use advertising or tracking cookies on this website. If we add analytics or advertising tools in the future, we will update this policy first.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and send you a quote or proposal.
  • To deliver, manage and report on the services you hire us for.
  • To send invoices, keep records and meet our legal obligations.
  • We never sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

  • Tools we use to run our business, such as WhatsApp, email and payment providers, only as needed.
  • Advertising platforms such as Meta and Google, when we run campaigns for you and you ask us to use your data there.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

Leads and customer data from your campaigns

Leads and customer data collected through your campaigns belong to you. We handle them only on your instructions and to deliver your service. You are responsible for having your customers’ consent where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep personal information only as long as we need it for the purpose it was shared, or as long as the law requires, for example for tax and accounting records. After that we delete it.

Security

Access to client information is limited to the team members working on your project. We use secure, access-controlled tools, but no method of storing or sending information online is completely secure.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the personal information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop using it, and you can withdraw any consent you have given. These rights apply under the laws that protect you, including India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and data protection laws in the UAE and other countries we work in.

Write to info@beezysolutions.com and we will reply within 30 days.

Children

Our services and this website are meant for businesses and are not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Other websites

This website links to our social media profiles, our Google reviews and WhatsApp. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version, with its date, is always on this page.
